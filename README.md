# Consumer-Complaint-Trends-and-Response-Intelligence-Platform
.1 Project Background
Financial institutions receive complaints from consumers about different financial products and services. These complaints may involve account management, credit cards, mortgages, credit reporting, payment services and other financial activities. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) collects consumer complaints and forwards eligible complaints to the relevant companies for review and response.
For this project, AnalystLab Africa is using consumer complaint records relating to Bank of America from 2017 to 2023 to simulate a professional financial services analytics engagement.
Important: A complaint represents an issue reported by a consumer. It does not automatically establish misconduct or prove that the company has violated any regulation. All findings are presented objectively and supported by the available data.
1.2 Problem Statement
Stakeholders require a reliable way to monitor consumer complaints and understand patterns in the complaints recorded against Bank of America. The project investigates complaint volumes, product and issue distributions, company response classifications, CFPB forwarding intervals, response timeliness, historical trends, and future complaint-volume forecasting.
1.3 Objectives
•	Monitor complaint volumes and trends.
•	Identify frequently reported financial products and issues.
•	Examine company response classifications.
•	Analyse CFPB complaint-forwarding intervals.
•	Investigate recorded response timeliness.
•	Explore historical trends and future complaint-volume forecasting.
•	Access validated complaint information through an interactive dashboard.
